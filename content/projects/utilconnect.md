## Summary
Utilities often run meter readings, customer records, tariffs, billing and payments in separate systems, and revenue slips through the gaps between them. UtilConnect is a single connected platform for that whole meter-to-cash lifecycle.

## My role
I'm the Founding Technical Lead at Proxta Global Systems, building UtilConnect remotely since July 2026:
- Own the technical architecture, product development and release management end to end
- Shape cloud, security and scalability decisions, and lead technical discovery and solution design with customers
- Set engineering standards and mentor the team as it grows

## What the platform covers
- **Customer 360:** accounts, premises, service agreements and history in one record
- **Meter data:** AMI, AMR, interval, manual and submeter reads, with validation, estimation and editing rules and exception handling
- **Tariffs and billing:** a configurable rating engine for time-of-use, tiered and thermal tariffs, bill runs, exception review, adjustments and ledgers
- **Customer portal:** bills, usage, payments and service requests
- **Revenue assurance:** monitoring for unbilled consumption, meter gaps and collection risk
- **Integrations:** APIs for AMI, ERP, GIS, CRM and payment systems, plus AI-assisted workflows

## Who it's for
Electric, water and wastewater, gas and district energy utilities, as well as commercial real estate and multifamily operators that bill for utilities.
